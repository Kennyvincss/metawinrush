skel.glb — SKEL, the METAWIN RUSH runner

Base mesh, rig and animations: "Universal Animation Library" (Standard) by Quaternius
https://quaternius.com  ·  License: CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain)
Source used: https://github.com/J-Ponzo/gltf-universal-animation-library (mirror of the itch.io release)

Trimmed to the clips the game uses (Sprint, Jog, Jump, Roll, Death, Hit, Idle, Dance) with
glTF-Transform. The Metawin outfit (helmet, visor, emblem, colours) is applied at runtime in
src/game/character3d.ts.
